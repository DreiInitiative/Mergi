Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Drei Initiative (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information in connection with Mergi (the “App” or “Game”), our mobile game for Android distributed through Google Play. We want you to understand what data is involved when you play, why it is involved, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Drei Initiative is the developer and data controller of the App. You can contact us at drei.contact.01@gmail.com. References in this policy to Google, Google Play, AdMob and related services are to Google LLC, whose own privacy practices are governed by its published policies.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the official App as distributed on Google Play, including its in-game advertising, in-app purchases, the daily challenge and global leaderboard server features, and locally stored game data. It does not cover practices by Google or other third parties that are outside our control; those are governed by the third parties’ own terms and privacy policies, which we link to below.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information stored only on your device

Mergi is designed so most of your data stays on your device. The following data is created and stored locally on your device: your player name, game progress and scores, unlocked achievements, purchased and selected cosmetic skins, your coin balance, and your audio, haptics and game-mode settings. This data is stored in the App’s private storage on your device, and deleting the App from your device erases it locally. A copy of the player name and scores you choose to publish to the global leaderboard is held on our server as described in Section 3.2.

3.2 Information we process for the daily challenge and leaderboard

To offer the daily challenge and the global leaderboard, we operate a server for the App. When you first use these features, the App generates a unique player identifier (a “UID”) that is stored on the server alongside the player name you choose. The server also stores the scores and daily challenge results you submit so that the leaderboard can be shown to all players. The daily challenge seed (a value used to generate each day’s puzzle) is delivered from the server to your device but is not linked to your player profile in an identifying way.

Your player name and leaderboard scores are visible to other players while you take part. Please choose a name that does not contain personal information about you, such as your real name, your email address or your phone number. The UID is a random identifier tied to the App on your device; we do not use it to track you across other apps, websites or services, and it is not combined with advertising or purchase data.

3.3 Information processed by advertising services

The App displays advertising — including banner ads and optional rewarded video ads — through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, Google may collect, use and share information about your device and your use of the App, which may include your advertising identifier, device identifiers and characteristics, coarse or approximate location derived from your IP address, the apps installed or used on your device, and interactions with ads. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted.

You can learn more about how Google handles information when you use mobile apps and sites that use Google services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and about Google’s privacy practices generally at policies.google.com/privacy.

3.4 Information processed for purchases

Coin purchases and other in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, process or store your payment card details or other financial information — those are handled by Google under the Google Payments Terms of Service and Google’s privacy policy. Google provides us with confirmation of completed transactions (such as the purchased item and order confirmation data) so the App can deliver the purchased coins to your device.

3.5 App permissions

The App requests only the permissions it needs to function:

  • Internet access and network state — required to load and show advertisements, to fetch daily-challenge seeds, and to submit and read leaderboard scores.
  • Vibration — used for short haptic feedback when orbs merge.
  • Storage write (Android 9 and older only) — used to save evolution videos when you choose to share a capture; on modern Android versions the App uses its own private storage instead.

3.6 Crash and stability diagnostics

To help keep the Game stable, the Android platform and Google may automatically collect technical diagnostic information related to crashes and stability (for example, the type of device, the Android version, and the circumstances of a crash), and make it available to us through the developer tools of Google Play. This information is processed under Google’s developer terms and privacy practices, is not used to identify you, and is not combined with your leaderboard data.

4. How information is used

  • To operate the Game, store your progress, and honor your settings (locally).
  • To operate the daily challenge and the global leaderboard, including storing and displaying your chosen player name and scores to other players (on our server).
  • To display ads, including rewarded ads that grant you in-game coins when you choose to watch them (via Google AdMob).
  • To deliver coins and other items you purchase through Google Play.
  • To maintain, improve and support the Game, including understanding crashes and stability issues through the reporting tools provided by the Android platform and Google.

5. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK)

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process information on the following bases: performance of a contract (operating the Game, delivering purchases you have paid for, and providing the daily challenge and leaderboard features you use); our legitimate interest in monetizing the App through advertising and in operating, securing and improving the leaderboard and daily challenge service; your consent, where required (for example, for personalized advertising on devices where Google requests it); and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing, as described in Section 7.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. The only third parties involved in the App’s operation are:

  • Google, through Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play Billing (purchases), each acting as an independent controller or processor of the data described in Sections 3.3 and 3.4, under Google’s policies linked above;
  • Google Play, which distributes the App itself; and
  • other players, who can see your chosen player name and leaderboard scores through the global leaderboard (the public leaderboard data described in Section 3.2).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share the UID, player name or leaderboard data with any third parties outside the App.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to enforce our rights in connection with fraud or abuse of the App or the Google Play platform.

7. Advertising choices and opting out

The App does not require an email, phone number or login of any kind. Apart from the player name and scores you choose to publish to the global leaderboard (Section 3.2) and the crash and stability diagnostics described in Section 3.6, your game data never leaves your device. For advertising-related data you have these options:

  • You can limit ad personalization on Android by opening your device’s Settings → Privacy → Ads and deleting or resetting your advertising ID, or by adjusting your Google Account’s Ads settings.
  • In regions where Google presents a consent flow before personalized advertising, you may accept or refuse personalization, and you can change your choice later in your device settings.
  • You can avoid rewarded ads entirely — they are always optional, and refusing them never blocks gameplay.

8. Data retention and security

Local game data is retained on your device until you uninstall the App. Leaderboard data (UID, player name and scores) is retained on our server for as long as you use the feature and we offer the Game; you may request its deletion at any time as described in Section 10. Data processed by Google in connection with advertising and purchases is retained and protected according to Google’s policies referenced above. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited leaderboard data stored on our server, and we do not request, store or transmit sensitive personal information.

9. Children’s privacy

The Game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed specifically at children under the age of 13, nor is it part of Google Play’s Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and where the age of digital consent is higher — in particular in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where it may be up to 16 — we do not knowingly collect personal information (including leaderboard player names and scores) from users below that age without parental consent. If children use the leaderboard, note that the player name and scores they publish are visible to other players, so a nickname that is not their real name is best. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information (for example, by contacting us), please write to drei.contact.01@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly and disable the affected player profile if applicable. Ad content shown in the App is supplied by Google AdMob and is subject to Google’s ad content standards.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Most game data lives on your device, so the simplest way to erase local data is to uninstall the App. Because leaderboard data is stored on our server, uninstalling the App does not remove it; you may ask us at any time to delete your player profile (UID, player name and leaderboard scores) by writing to drei.contact.01@gmail.com and we will do so promptly. For anything processed through Google services, the controls described in Section 7 and Google’s own account tools apply. In all cases you may also contact us at drei.contact.01@gmail.com and we will assist you. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

11. International transfers

Where information is processed by Google, it may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. Such transfers are governed by Google’s policies and the transfer mechanisms described in them, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable. Your leaderboard data is stored on servers operated by our hosting provider, which may be located outside your country; where the GDPR applies, that data is processed under appropriate safeguards, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, when the App gains new features or when our service providers change. The current version is always available at this page, and material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
